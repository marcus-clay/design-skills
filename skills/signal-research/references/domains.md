# Domain Taxonomy and Seed Queries

9 domain clusters for the Signal Research skill. Each has a description, seed search queries and priority sources. Append the current year to queries for recency.

---

## Domain 1: Post-Figma Toolchain

**What this covers:** The fragmentation and evolution of design tooling after Figma's dominance. Includes: AI-native design tools (Galileo, Uizard, Motiff), code-first design (Framer, Webflow, v0, Bolt, Lovable), MCP integrations with Figma, the viability of Figma in agentic workflows, the rise of "design in the browser" as default.

**Seed queries:**
- `Figma alternatives AI design tools`
- `post-Figma era design workflow`
- `v0 Bolt Lovable design process`
- `Figma MCP model context protocol`
- `design tool fragmentation`
- `AI native design tools Galileo Motiff`

**Priority sources:** Config talks (Figma), Figma blog, Framer blog, Vercel blog, UX Collective Substack, Design Tools Survey (results)

---

## Domain 2: AI-Assisted Design Workflows (Real, Not Demo)

**What this covers:** How practitioners are actually integrating AI into design work: shipping workflows beyond concept demos. Includes: LLMs in UX research synthesis, AI in design systems (token generation, documentation), prompt-driven prototyping, AI in accessibility audits, AI-assisted user testing, vibe coding by non-developers, coding agents combined with Figma MCP in production.

**Seed queries:**
- `AI UX research synthesis workflow`
- `designers using LLMs production workflow`
- `AI design system documentation automation`
- `prompt-driven prototyping real workflow`
- `AI accessibility audit tools`
- `vibe coding designers non-developers`
- `coding agent Figma MCP design workflow`
- `AI usability testing production`

**Priority sources:** Lenny's Newsletter, Every.to, UX Collective, Nielsen Norman Group reports, Smashing Magazine, individual practitioner posts on LinkedIn

## Domain 3: Design Leadership and Org Design

**What this covers:** The changing role of design in organizations. Includes: the shrinking of design teams at tech companies, design ops evolution, the PM-designer boundary blurring, design maturity models, Head of Design vs. CPO dynamics, embedded vs. centralized design org structures, hiring freezes and their effect on design career paths, the rise of the fractional/consulting design director model.

**Seed queries:**
- `design team layoffs restructuring`
- `design ops role evolution`
- `Head of Design role changing`
- `PM designer boundary blurring product`
- `design maturity model enterprise`
- `embedded design centralized design org`
- `fractional design director consulting model`

**Priority sources:** Design Leadership Forum, Org Design for Design Orgs (book discourse), Nielsen Norman Group, Figma's State of Design report, LinkedIn posts from design VPs

## Domain 4: B2B / B2G / SaaS UX

**What this covers:** Design practice specific to enterprise, government and complex SaaS products. Includes: accessibility as procurement condition (national standards such as RGAA in France, EN 301 549 in the EU, Section 508 in the US), complex data visualization, admin UX, multi-tenant SaaS design, design for regulated industries, public sector digital transformation, GovTech UX standards, national government design systems.

**Seed queries:**
- `B2B SaaS UX design patterns`
- `accessibility procurement requirements public sector`
- `EN 301 549 EU accessibility enforcement`
- `enterprise UX design complexity`
- `GovTech public sector UX`
- `admin dashboard design patterns`
- `multi-tenant SaaS design system`
- `government design system evolution`

**Priority sources:** Baymard Institute, GOV.UK design system blog, national government design system sites, Nielsen Norman Group enterprise UX reports, Intercom design blog

## Domain 5: Design Process Automation

**What this covers:** Automating repetitive or low-value design tasks. Includes: automated design QA, style guide generation from code, automated component documentation, CI/CD for design systems, design token pipelines, automated accessibility testing in CI, AI-driven usability testing (Maze, Useberry, Lyssna with AI overlays), AI-assisted meeting transcription and synthesis in design workflows.

**Seed queries:**
- `design system automation CI CD`
- `automated design QA tools`
- `design token pipeline automation`
- `automated accessibility testing CI`
- `AI usability testing Maze Useberry`
- `Storybook automated documentation`
- `AI meeting transcription design research`

**Priority sources:** Storybook blog, Tokens Studio, Style Dictionary docs, design systems Slack communities (public posts), Knapsack, Supernova

## Domain 6: AI Strategy and Product Design Intersection

**What this covers:** Designing AI-powered products: products where AI is the feature, beyond tools for designers. Includes: AI UX patterns (confidence indicators, error states, progressive disclosure of AI), designing for hallucination, conversational UI patterns, agentic UX (when the product acts autonomously), ethical AI design, onboarding for AI features, multi-agent product architectures and what they mean for UX.

**Seed queries:**
- `AI UX patterns design`
- `agentic UX design autonomous agents`
- `conversational UI design patterns`
- `designing for AI uncertainty hallucination`
- `AI onboarding UX best practices`
- `multi-agent product UX design`
- `ethical AI design product`

**Priority sources:** Nielsen Norman Group (AI UX research), Microsoft Research HCI, Google PAIR Explorables, ACM CHI proceedings, Anthropic design blog, OpenAI design blog

## Domain 7: Obsolete vs. Surviving Processes

**What this covers:** Which traditional UX/design methods are dying, which are adapting, which remain essential. Includes: the fate of personas, user journey maps, usability testing methods, design sprints (Google Design Sprint discourse), waterfall-to-agile-to-continuous-discovery transitions, card sorting and tree testing in an AI era, heuristic evaluation vs. AI audits.

**Seed queries:**
- `UX methods obsolete AI`
- `design sprint dead critique`
- `personas outdated alternative`
- `continuous discovery vs design sprint`
- `usability testing AI automation`
- `UX research methods survive AI`

**Priority sources:** Teresa Torres (continuous discovery), Jared Spool (UIE), Nielsen Norman Group, Product Talk newsletter, UX Research community posts

## Domain 8: Design x Emerging Tech Frontiers

**What this covers:** Design practice at the edge of new computing paradigms. Includes: spatial computing UX (Vision Pro, AR/VR), voice and multimodal UI, wearable UX, design for low-bandwidth/offline-first, designing for emerging markets, climate-conscious design (digital sobriety), dark patterns regulation (EU DSA).

**Seed queries:**
- `spatial computing UX design`
- `Vision Pro app design patterns`
- `multimodal UI design voice gesture`
- `EU DSA dark patterns regulation design`
- `digital sobriety eco-design`
- `offline-first UX design`

**Priority sources:** Apple Human Interface Guidelines (visionOS), Nielsen Norman Group spatial UX, EU Commission DSA enforcement updates, Sustainable Web Design community

---

## Domain 9: AI Regulation as Design Constraint

**What this covers:** How AI regulation (the EU AI Act in particular, plus equivalents elsewhere) is becoming an active constraint on product and UX design decisions. Includes: risk classification and what it means for product teams, transparency and explainability requirements as UX patterns, human oversight obligations in high-risk AI systems, CE marking for AI, GDPR + AI Act overlap in data-driven UX, what regulators expect from "human-in-the-loop" design, AI compliance as a procurement condition in the public sector.

**Seed queries:**
- `EU AI Act UX design compliance`
- `AI Act transparency requirements product design`
- `human oversight AI systems UX patterns`
- `EU AI Act high-risk systems product teams`
- `GDPR AI Act design implications`
- `AI Act public procurement compliance design`
- `explainable AI UX requirements regulation`

**Priority sources:** European Commission AI Act documentation, ENISA AI guidelines, national data protection authority guidance on AI, Future of Privacy Forum, AlgorithmWatch, Access Now, practitioner posts on AI compliance from the design/legal intersection
